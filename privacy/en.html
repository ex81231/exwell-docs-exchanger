1.1.3. “Processing of personal data” means any operation or set of operations performed on personal data, whether or not by automated means, including collection, recording, organisation, accumulation, storage, updating (alteration), retrieval, use, disclosure (dissemination, provision, access), anonymisation, blocking, erasure, and destruction of personal data.

1.1.6. “IP address” means a unique network address of a device connected to the Internet and identified under the IP protocol.

3. Subject of the Policy

3.1. The Policy sets out the Site Administration’s obligations to protect personal data provided by the User upon registration on the Site, when using the personal account and the Business API, and in other cases at the Site Administration’s request.


3.2. Upon registration, the User provides, in particular, an email address and a password, as well as other information required to access the account. When using the personal account and/or the Business API, the following additional categories of data may be processed:


a) a TRON network payment address issued for making prepayment;

3.3. The User’s personal data are processed confidentially and are not disclosed to third parties, except as expressly provided by this Policy or applicable law.
